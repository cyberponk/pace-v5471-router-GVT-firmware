NTP Server

Sets the router's clock from Internet time servers (NTP) at every connection and once a day, and the time zone used for the logs and the schedules. HTTPS (dynamic DNS, DNS over TLS) needs the right date to check certificates.

Settings
Time synchronization:On: the clock is set from the servers below. Off: the clock starts at a fixed date at every boot.
NTP servers:Up to 4 time servers, by name or IP address, separated by commas (default 0.pool.ntp.org, 1.pool.ntp.org, 2.pool.ntp.org; a.ntp.br is the Brazilian one). The interface follows the operating mode: the local network in access point mode, the Internet connection in router mode.
Time zone:Local time shown by the router. Brazil has no daylight saving time since 2019.
Time zone (POSIX):Name and offset west of UTC, optionally the summer time name and rules, for example BRT3 or CET-1CEST,M3.5.0,M10.5.0/3.
