USB Tethering
In router mode, an Android phone plugged into the USB port with USB tethering on is kept ready as a backup Internet connection. While the WAN connection is down (no connection, or no answer from the Internet), all traffic goes through the phone; when the WAN answers again the router switches back. Switching takes about 15 seconds and open connections are restarted.
USB tethering failover:Enabled: in router mode the phone takes over automatically while the WAN connection is down. Disabled: the phone is not used.
